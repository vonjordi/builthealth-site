Every other tracker silently guesses what you ate. We ask.

Point the camera at a plate and most apps hand you a confident number pulled out of thin air. Built Health runs an accuracy engine instead: it identifies what's actually there, asks the one question that matters, and shows you the evidence behind every gram — with an honest range instead of false precision.

One Lens

One camera. Everything it needs to know.

No mode-picking. Point it at a plate, a barcode, a nutrition label, or even a restaurant receipt — it figures out what it's looking at and routes itself.

  • Barcodes read live, as you point
  • Labels beat databases, automatically
  • Receipts become itemized meals
  • Or hold the mic and just say what you ate
The evidence trail

Numbers you can audit.

Tap any meal to see exactly where its numbers came from: which source won, what the range is, and which items still need a second look. Protein leads, because muscle is what keeps you strong for decades.

  • Honest ranges, never fake precision
  • Per-ingredient breakdown and sources
  • Conflicts surfaced instead of hidden
  • It learns your portions over time
Medications & vitamins

Never wonder if you took it.

Add your meds and supplements — or photograph the bottle and let the app read it. Built Health builds the schedule, reminds you at the right times, flags what you missed, and estimates when your refill runs out.

  • Timing intelligence: with food, empty stomach, weekly
  • Missed-dose banner the moment you open the app
  • Refill countdown before you run out
Symptoms & your Coach

Tell a bad day from a bad pattern.

Log how you feel in seconds and watch the month fill in. Patterns stop hiding, and your Coach keeps the context — so you walk into appointments with a record instead of a fuzzy memory.

  • Calendar view of what flares and when
  • A private journal that remembers across months
  • Questions worth asking your doctor
