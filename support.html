A scan looked wrong?

Barcode databases are sometimes wrong — that's exactly why Built Health never silently trusts them. Scan the product's nutrition label with the same camera: the label always overrides the barcode, and the app remembers the correction.

The mic button isn't showing?

Voice features need microphone and speech-recognition permission. If you declined them, re-enable both for Built Health in your phone's Settings, then reopen the app.

Deleting your data

You can delete any meal, diary entry, medication, or symptom in the app. For full account deletion, email privacy@builthealth.com from the address linked to your account.

A note on health

Built Health is a wellness tracker, not a medical service. It never diagnoses or gives treatment advice. For anything concerning — new, sharp, or persistent symptoms — contact your doctor or local telehealth line.
