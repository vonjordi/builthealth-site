Delete your Built Health account

This page explains how to delete your Built Health account and the data associated with it. Built Health is published by InspiraCube (1001474709 Ontario Inc.).

Request account deletion

  1. Send an email to vonjordi@gmail.com with the subject line "Delete my Built Health account".
  2. Send it from the email address you use to sign in to Built Health, so we can verify the request is really yours.
  3. We will confirm and permanently delete your account and associated data within 30 days.

What gets deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes your sign-in credentials and all data linked to your account: meal logs and their evidence records, medication and vitamin schedules and dose history, symptom logs, diary entries and derived memory, recipes and taste preferences, and profile settings.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Purchase and billing records are handled by Google Play and RevenueCat and are retained by those services under their own policies; we keep only what is legally required for accounting.
  • Server logs that may reference your account ID are retained for up to 30 days for security and abuse prevention, then deleted automatically.
  • Meal photos are never retained — they are processed and discarded at scan time.

Delete data without deleting your account

Inside the app you can delete individual records at any time — meals, medications, symptom entries, diary entries (or all diary data at once), and recipes — without deleting your account.
